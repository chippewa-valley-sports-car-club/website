The club runs on volunteers and local businesses. Sponsorship keeps autocross entry fees low, buys cones, timing gear and insurance, and funds what the club gives back: autocross proceeds pay for two [CVTC automotive scholarships](../community/cvtc-scholarships/) every year, and members coach at the [Teen Car Control Class](../community/teen-car-control/).

### $500 for the season

There's one sponsorship, and it covers the whole season. You get:

- Regular advertising on our Facebook page and mailing list
- Dedicated space at both of our yearly autocrosses, for cars, tables, signage or whatever suits your business
- Shoutouts at our in-person events, and whenever we broadcast an event on social media
- Thank-yous at our club meetings
- Your logo on this website, linked to your site

Club members also send work to the businesses that back the club. Depending on your business, those referrals can pay back the $500 on their own.

### Get in touch

Ask any officer at a meeting, or reach us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/d3kfjmWDQ8) or through the [Contact](../contact/) page. We'll tell you which events are coming up and how to send a logo.
