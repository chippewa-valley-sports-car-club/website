Got footage from a club event? Send it in and we may post it to the club's YouTube, TikTok and Instagram, with credit to you.

## What works best

- **Whole runs**, from the start line to the finish, not just the good bit. We cut the teaser.
- **Landscape**, with the phone mounted solidly to the car (a suction or clamp mount, not held in a hand).
- **Original files** at full quality, straight off the phone or camera.

## The fine print

By submitting, you confirm that you shot the footage and that the club may post it, edited, to its YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts with credit to you.
