The club runs on volunteers and local businesses. Sponsorship keeps autocross entry fees low, buys cones, timing gear and insurance, and funds what the club gives back: autocross proceeds pay for two [CVTC automotive scholarships](../community/cvtc-scholarships/) every year, and members coach at the [Teen Car Control Class](../community/teen-car-control/).

There are two ways to sponsor. Pick one, or both.

### Club sponsors: the whole season

For businesses that want to be part of the club all year.

| Tier | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** (one per season) | Top of this page and first on the home page; your logo on the club's event banners; thanked at every event and in the season's social posts; a member offer listed for the year |
| **Gold** | Your logo on the home page and this page; a member offer listed; thanked at events |
| **Supporter** | Your name and link on this page; a member offer listed |

### Event sponsors: one event

For businesses that want to back a single autocross, track day or social.

| Tier | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **Presenting** (one per event) | "*Event name*, presented by *you*" on the event page, results and posts; a banner at the event; first among that event's sponsors |
| **Event** | Your logo on the event page and its results; thanked at the drivers' meeting |
| **In-kind** | Products, prizes, food or services in place of money; listed on the event page |

### Get in touch

Ask any officer at a meeting, or reach us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/d3kfjmWDQ8) or through the [Contact](../contact/) page. We'll tell you the current rates, which events are coming up, and how to send a logo.
