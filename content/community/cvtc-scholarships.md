Every year the Chippewa Valley Sports Car Club funds two scholarships for automotive students at **Chippewa Valley Technical College (CVTC)**. The money comes from autocross entry fees and membership dues, so every driver who runs one of our autocrosses helps pay for them.

## The scholarships

The club funds two memorial scholarships through the CVTC Foundation:

- **The Doug Christensen Memorial Scholarship**
- **The Dick Feeney Memorial Scholarship**

One goes to a student in the **Auto Collision Repair & Refinish Technician** program and one to a student in the **Automotive Maintenance Technician** program.

## Students: how to apply

Applications go through CVTC's scholarship site, not the club. See the club's current scholarships, award amounts and deadlines on [CVTC's scholarship page for the Chippewa Valley Sports Car Club](https://cvtc.academicworks.com/donors/chippewa-valley-sports-car-club). The deadline has fallen in mid-February in recent years.

## Help fund them

Run an autocross, join the club, or [sponsor an event](../../sponsors/). Proceeds from our events go to these scholarships.
