The club's autocrosses run at **[Rock Falls Raceway](https://www.rockfallsraceway.com/)**, Eau Claire's drag strip, about 9 miles south of town off Highway 85 near Caryville. A big thank-you to the track for hosting us.

Rock Falls opened in May 1969 as Amber Green Dragway. In the late 2010s the track put in new barriers, resurfaced the strip and built a new tower, and every year it gives the Badger Classic's proceeds to a local charity.

## Try drag racing

You don't need a race car. Rock Falls' **Test & Tune** days are open to street cars, and they're a low-pressure way to run your own car down the strip. You'll need an approved helmet and the right clothing; the track can rent you a helmet.

[The track's Test & Tune page](https://www.rockfallsraceway.com/daytest) lists what to bring, what it costs and when the gates open. Check it before you go, since the details change from season to season.

## Big events at the track

Rock Falls runs drag racing most weekends through the season, from bracket racing and the ET Series to big weekends like the Badger Classic, the NHRA National Open, the Fallout Drags and the Mopar Showdown. The track posts this year's dates on its [events page](https://www.rockfallsraceway.com/events), along with its [racer handbook](https://www.rockfallsraceway.com/handbook) and [FAQ](https://www.rockfallsraceway.com/contact/faq).
