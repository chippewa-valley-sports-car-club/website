*Effective October 22, 2026.*

These terms cover the Chippewa Valley Sports Car Club's website and the club's video publishing app.

- **The website** is provided as-is for information about the club. Event details can change; the event's own registration page is the final word.
- **The video publishing app** is operated by club officers to post the club's own videos to the club's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts. It is not offered to the public.
- **Videos you submit** stay yours. By submitting one, you confirm you shot it and give the club permission to edit it and post it with credit on the club's accounts. Ask us on Discord to take one down.
- **Motorsport is dangerous.** Nothing on this site is driving instruction. Drive fast only at organized events, never on public roads.

Questions? Ask the club on Discord, or through the [Contact](../contact/) page.
