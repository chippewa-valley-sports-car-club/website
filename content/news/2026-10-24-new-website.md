The club has a new website, with everything in one place:

- **Results:** full 2026 autocross results with raw times, PAX and every class.
- **Events:** the calendar, and a feed you can subscribe to in Google or Apple Calendar, plus links to everything else running near Eau Claire on MotorsportReg.
- **Our history:** from the first rally out of Chippewa Falls in 1957 to today.
- **Giving back:** the Teen Car Control Class and the club's two CVTC scholarships.
- **Rock Falls Raceway:** our home track, and how to try their Test & Tune days.

It's still filling in. The club's own address, www.cvscc.org, moves over to this site once the DNS switchover is done; until then, this address works, and it will keep working afterwards.
