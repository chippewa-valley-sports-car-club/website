The Chippewa Valley Sports Car Club is a group of car people from the Eau Claire area. Some of us race, some of us wrench, and some of us just like being around interesting cars. Bring whatever you drive, whatever your experience.

## What we do

- **Autocross:** timed runs through a cone course in a parking lot. It's the cheapest, safest way to find out what your car can do, and most of us started here.
- **Track days:** high-performance driving events at road courses around the Midwest, with instruction for newcomers.
- **Rally:** rallycross, time-speed-distance rallies and stage rally.
- **Sim racing:** Assetto Corsa practice with Fugazy Sim Racing when the weather won't cooperate.
- **Social events:** meetings, drives and get-togethers.

## Our history

The club started informally in the 1950s, when MG-TD drivers in the area began getting together. They ran their first rally out of Chippewa Falls in 1957.

Jack Nozle pushed the group to organize, and in 1960 Bill Bean, Bob Plunkett and Dick Feeney laid the groundwork. They held the first meeting on April 20, 1960, at the old Import Motors building on Hwy 93, borrowing the Twin Cities Sports Car Club's bylaws, and most of the people there signed on as charter members. Thirteen cars ran the first event, a hare-and-hound rally out of the Chippewa Falls fairgrounds.

Through the 1960s and 70s the club ran rallies and gymkhanas, including ice gymkhanas on Altoona Lake and one at Carson Park, along with the Alpine Rallye, the Parker's Peak Hillclimb and the Northwoods Rally, and made the trip to Road America. The energy crunch and the 55 mph speed limit put the club to sleep in 1980.

Twenty years later, Dick Feeney and Jim McCarthy got talking at the B.K. Auto Show in the fall of 2000. Meetings started again at O'Leary Pub in Eau Claire in January 2001, and the club officially came back that April.

Today the [Dick Feeney Memorial Scholarship](../community/cvtc-scholarships/) carries one founder's name.

## In the community

Club members provide most of the in-car coaches for the [Teen Car Control Class](../community/teen-car-control/), and our autocross proceeds fund two [CVTC automotive scholarships](../community/cvtc-scholarships/) every year.

## Where we run

Our autocrosses run at [Rock Falls Raceway](../community/rock-falls-raceway/), Eau Claire's drag strip, where members also race at its Test & Tune days.

## Meetings

We meet quarterly at Hangar 54. You'll find the next date on the [home page](../), and we announce it on Discord.
