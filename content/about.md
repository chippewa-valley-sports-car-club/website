The Chippewa Valley Sports Car Club is a group of car people from the Eau Claire area. Some of us race, some of us wrench, and some of us just like being around interesting cars. Any car and any experience level are welcome.

## What we do

- **Autocross:** timed runs through a cone course in a parking lot. It's the cheapest, safest way to find out what your car can do, and most of us started here.
- **Track days:** high-performance driving events at road courses around the Midwest, with instruction for newcomers.
- **Rally:** rallycross, time-speed-distance rallies and stage rally.
- **Sim racing:** Assetto Corsa practice with Fugazy Sim Racing when the weather won't cooperate.
- **Social events:** meetings, drives and get-togethers.

## In the community

Club members provide most of the in-car coaches for the [Teen Car Control Class](../community/teen-car-control/), and our autocross proceeds fund two [CVTC automotive scholarships](../community/cvtc-scholarships/) every year. The club has been part of the Chippewa Valley for over 60 years.

## Meetings

We meet quarterly at Hangar 54. The date of the next one is on the [home page](../) and announced on Discord.
