---
title: Join the club
---

Anyone can be a member. Here's how:

1. **Come to the next meeting.** We meet quarterly at Hangar 54; the next date is on the [home page](../). Introduce yourself and an officer will add you as a member.
2. **Join the Discord.** It's where event plans, carpools, results and buy/sell happen. When you join, pick the kinds of events you're into so you only get pinged about those.

You don't need a fast car or any experience. Bring whatever you drive.
