title: Privacy policy
---

*Effective October 22, 2026.*

This policy covers the Chippewa Valley Sports Car Club's website and the club's video publishing app, which posts the club's own videos to its own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts.

## The website

The website collects no information about you. It has no accounts, forms, cookies, analytics or advertising. GitHub Pages hosts it, and GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses), as [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) describes.

## The video publishing app

Only club officers use the app, and only to publish videos to the club's own accounts. It collects no data about viewers or other users of YouTube, TikTok or Instagram. We keep the access tokens those platforms issue to it only on the club's own computer, use them only to post the club's videos, and never share or sell them.

## Video submissions

When you send the club a video, we keep your name and social handle to credit you when we post it. Ask us on Discord and we'll remove a submission or your details.

## Questions

Ask the club on Discord, or through the [Contact](../contact/) page.
