title: Privacy policy
---

*Effective October 22, 2026.*

This policy covers the Chippewa Valley Sports Car Club's website and the club's video publishing app, which posts the club's own videos to its own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts.

## The website

The website collects no information about you. It has no accounts, forms, cookies, analytics or advertising. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) as described in [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## The video publishing app

The app is used only by club officers to publish videos to the club's own accounts. It does not collect data about viewers or other users of YouTube, TikTok or Instagram. The access tokens those platforms issue to it are stored only on the club's own computer, used only to post the club's videos, and never shared or sold.

## Video submissions

When you send the club a video, we keep your name and social handle to credit you when we post it. Ask us on Discord to have a submission or your details removed.

## Questions

Ask the club on Discord, or through the [Contact](../contact/) page.
